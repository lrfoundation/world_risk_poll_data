# Reports on the World Risk Poll 2023 (wave 3)

The 2023 poll: fieldwork in 2023, 146,910 respondents in 142 countries and areas. Its reports were published as the *World Risk Poll 2024* reports (four core reports, 2024) and two Focus On reports (2025 and 2026). *Migration in a warming world* is published in the [Global Safety Evidence Centre library](https://repo.gsec.org.uk) (DOI [10.60743/5ppn-nc25](https://doi.org/10.60743/5ppn-nc25)) rather than on the Lloyd's Register Foundation publications pages.

Data: [`WRP_2023/`](../../WRP_2023/). Each folder below reproduces one report in Python and R; see [`reports/README.md`](../README.md) for how to run them and the conventions they share.
